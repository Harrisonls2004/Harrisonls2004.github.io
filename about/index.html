    TACL submit

    LLM-Native Topic Modeling: Grounding Recovery via Decode–Infer Surrogates and Long-Input Evaluation

    共一作者 TACL (拟投稿)

    Xuan Xu*, Zhongliang Yang*, Haolun Li*, Beilin Chu, Rui Tian, Yu Li, Shaolin Tan, Linna Zhou†
    • 将主题建模形式化为结构化长输入生成任务，由LLM统一生成主题表述与代表文档。

    • 在黑盒场景下，面向闭源API模型构建基于主题名称、摘要和关键词的检索代理，实现稳定的文档-主题分配。
    • 提出面向LLM的生成式主题建模流程，并设计主题质量、语义一致性等评测指标；实验表明该方法在主题可读性和文档分配上优于传统神经主题模型与常见聚类式方法。

    ACL 2026 Findings

    Towards Modern Topic Models: A Survey of Taxonomies and Paradigm Shifts from Algorithm-Centric to LLM-Centered Topic Analysis

    Co-first Author ACL 2026 Findings (accepted)

    Xuan Xu*, Zhongliang Yang*, Haolun Li*, Rui Tian, Beilin Chu, J Song, Yu Li, Shaolin Tan, Linna Zhou†
    • Constructed a unified taxonomy that categorizes topic modeling approaches into Algorithm-Centric, LLM-Assisted, and LLM-Centric paradigms.
    • Systematically analyzed representative technical approaches including prompting methods, two-stage generation, and multi-agent architectures from task workflow and system design perspectives.
    • Identified key challenges in long-context modeling, evaluation frameworks, and interpretability, providing a systematic research survey for the field.
    • Revealed the critical impact of LLMs on the topic modeling paradigm shift from probabilistic modeling to semantic and generation-driven analysis.
    TACL submit

    LLM-Native Topic Modeling: Grounding Recovery via Decode–Infer Surrogates and Long-Input Evaluation

    Co-first Author TACL (planned submission)

    Xuan Xu*, Zhongliang Yang*, Haolun Li*, Beilin Chu, Rui Tian, Yu Li, Shaolin Tan, Linna Zhou†
    • Formalized topic modeling as a structured long-input generation task, where LLMs jointly generate topic descriptions and representative documents.
    • In white-box settings, recovered document-topic and topic-word associations from attention signals during open-source model generation.
    • In black-box settings, built a retrieval surrogate over topic names, summaries, and keywords for robust document-topic assignment with closed API models.
    • Proposed an LLM-native generative topic modeling workflow and evaluation metrics for topic quality and semantic consistency; experiments show stronger readability and assignment performance than traditional NTMs and clustering-based methods.
