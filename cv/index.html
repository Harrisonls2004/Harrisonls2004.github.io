    ACL 2026 Findings

    Towards Modern Topic Models: A Survey of Taxonomies and Paradigm Shifts from Algorithm-Centric to LLM-Centered Topic Analysis

    Co-first Author
    ACL 2026 Findings (accepted)

    Xuan Xu*, Zhongliang Yang*, Haolun Li*, Rui Tian, Beilin Chu, J Song, Yu Li, Shaolin Tan, Linna Zhou†- Constructed a unified taxonomy that categorizes topic modeling approaches into Algorithm-Centric, LLM-Assisted, and LLM-Centric paradigms.

    • Systematically analyzed representative technical approaches including prompting methods, two-stage generation, and multi-agent architectures from task workflow and system design perspectives.
    • Identified key challenges in long-context modeling, evaluation frameworks, and interpretability, providing a systematic research survey for the field.
    • Revealed the critical impact of LLMs on the topic modeling paradigm shift from probabilistic modeling to semantic and generation-driven analysis.
    TACL submit

    LLM-Native Topic Modeling: Grounding Recovery via Decode–Infer Surrogates and Long-Input Evaluation

    Co-first Author
    TACL (planned submission)

    Xuan Xu*, Zhongliang Yang*, Haolun Li*, Beilin Chu, Rui Tian, Yu Li, Shaolin Tan, Linna Zhou†- Formalized topic modeling as a structured long-input generation task, where LLMs jointly generate topic descriptions and representative documents.

    • In white-box settings, recovered document-topic and topic-word associations from attention signals during open-source model generation.
    • In black-box settings, built a retrieval surrogate over topic names, summaries, and keywords for robust document-topic assignment with closed API models.
    • Proposed an LLM-native generative topic modeling workflow and evaluation metrics for topic quality and semantic consistency; experiments show stronger readability and assignment performance than traditional NTMs and clustering-based methods.

    AgentMark: A Utility-Preserving Behavioral Watermarking Framework for LLM Agents

    Core Developer
    2026.01

    Code

    • Designed and implemented AgentMark, a utility-preserving behavioral watermarking framework for LLM agents, addressing the lack of effective behavior provenance, copyright attribution, and audit trail in black-box agent operations.
    • Proposed a method to embed multi-bit watermark identifiers into the agent’s planning and decision-making process, enabling implicit behavioral trajectory marking, provenance verification, and copyright protection without modifying model weights or compromising task performance.
    • Integrated behavior distribution sampling, robust encoding, log collection, and visualization-based monitoring modules, and validated the framework’s watermark capacity, robustness, and utility preservation across multiple agent environments including ALFWorld and ToolBench.
